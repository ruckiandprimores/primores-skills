# The flags and why they're set where they are

These are rules of thumb for small accounts, not Meta's rules. They're tuned to avoid the most common small-business mistake: pausing ads after one bad day, and leaving ads on that quietly spend for a week with nothing to show.

| Flag | When | Why this threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Consider pausing | 0 results in the last 7 days and 7-day spend at least 2× the target cost per result | One target's worth of spend with nothing can be bad luck. Two in a week with nothing is a pattern. |
| Watch today | 0 results yesterday and yesterday's spend at least 1× target | Worth a look, not an action. Meta reports conversions with a delay and days vary. |
| Cost rising | Yesterday's cost per result more than 1.5× the ad's own 7-day cost per result, with at least 3 results in 7 days | Compares the ad to itself, so a pricier audience doesn't get flagged for being pricier. The 3-result minimum stops one expensive result from looking like a trend. |
| Expensive on few results | 1 or 2 results in 7 days, each costing at least 2× target | Not zero, so the pause rule misses it, but each result cost double what it's worth. Two results is too few to call it, so it's a watch, not a pause. |
| Doing well | 7-day cost per result at or below 0.7× target, with at least 3 results, and cost not rising yesterday | Clearly better than target, on enough results to trust. An ad whose cost jumped yesterday goes under Watch instead, so no ad shows up twice. |
| Tired audience | 7-day frequency 3.5 or higher | The same people have seen it three or four times in a week; results usually slide after that for cold audiences. Retargeting ads run higher on purpose; say so if the ad set name suggests retargeting. |
| Learning limited | Ad set status "Learning limited" | Meta says the ad set isn't getting enough results to finish learning. Usual fixes: a bigger budget, a broader audience, or fewer ad sets sharing the budget. |
| Spend jump | Account spend yesterday at least 25% above or below its 7-day daily average | Usually a budget change someone made, a new ad set, or one ad set delivering much more or less than usual. Worth one line so it isn't a surprise on the invoice. |
| Not spending | Yesterday's spend under half the ad set's daily budget | Something is holding delivery back: audience too small, bid cap too low, or the ad is in review. |

**No target given:** the script uses the account's own 7-day cost per result as the target. That makes "doing well" and "consider pausing" relative to the account's average, not to what the business can afford. Say this in the report and ask for a real number.

**Mixed result types:** cost per lead and cost per purchase can't share one target. Check each campaign type separately.
